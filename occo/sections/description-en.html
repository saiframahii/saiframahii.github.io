Occupant-Centric Control Ontology (OCCO): Description back to ToC

OCCO was developed as part of PhD research at Birmingham City University. It provides a shared semantic model for occupant-centric control research, with lighting as the primary application domain. By establishing a common vocabulary for sensor observations, occupant feedback, control strategies, and performance evaluation, OCCO enables OCC research findings to be consistently described, compared, and built upon. The version presented here accompanies the initial EC3 2026 conference submission and will continue to evolve as validation studies are conducted.
